Home Board

A shared board for an apartment that runs full screen on a Raspberry Pi: a big clock, a grocery list, the weather, sticky notes, a calendar and a few games. Everyone at home can use it from the touchscreen or from their phone.

It is free and open source, it runs on your own hardware, and it keeps its data on that hardware. There is no hosted service and no account.
